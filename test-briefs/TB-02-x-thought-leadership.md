# TB-02: X Thought Leadership

**Topic:** Hot take: companies adopt async tools without changing meeting habits — then wonder why it doesn't work.

**Tone note:** Founder voice. Direct, confident, slightly provocative. No CTA. Ends with a question or open statement.

## Expected output

- Single tweet (max 280 chars) or thread of 3–4 tweets
- First tweet stands alone and is share-worthy
- No more than 1 hashtag
- Ends with something that invites a reply or retweet
- Feels like a founder's take, not a brand post

## Validation criteria

- First tweet ≤ 280 characters
- Has a Loopin-specific perspective (not generic)
- Founder voice: direct, not corporate

## Sofia revision scenario

**First attempt (too generic):**
- "Tools aren't the problem, habits are"
- "Async adoption needs culture change"

**Reviewer feedback:** "These angles are too broad. Show me the Loopin perspective. Be more direct."

**Second attempt (should improve):**
- "You can't Loopin your way out of a meeting culture..."
- "The companies winning async are the ones that killed meeting defaults..."
- "Loopin + broken habits = expensive tool collecting dust..."
