# TB-05: LinkedIn Data Post

**Topic:** 23 hours/week in meetings (HBR, 2017 — Perlow, Hadley & Eun). Build a credible case around the real cost of meetings.

**Structure:** stat → cost implication → alternative → CTA

**Source to cite:** HBR 2017 (Perlow, Hadley & Eun)

**Tone note:** Insight post, not a rant. Credible and grounded. Slightly uncomfortable in a good way — reader should question their own habits.

## Expected output

- LinkedIn post, 900–1400 characters
- Opens with the 23hr stat in a scroll-stopping way
- Includes cost implication (time × salary)
- Proposes async as the alternative
- One CTA (e.g. "How does your week compare?")
- HBR source cited in caption or comments
- Visual: clean stat graphic

## Validation criteria

- 23hr stat appears in first 2 lines
- Cost implication quantified
- Async proposed as alternative (not just "fewer meetings")
- CTA is a question or reflection prompt
- Source cited
