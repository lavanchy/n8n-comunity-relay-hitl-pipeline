# TB-04: Instagram Culture Post

**Topic:** Day in the life of Mia, product designer in Lisbon — fully async, no meetings.

**Mia's day:**
- Morning video update to team
- 20 min reviewing async updates
- 4 hrs deep work
- Design review video at 2pm
- Done at 5pm

**Tone note:** Visual-first. Warm, real, not staged. Caption adds context — conversational tone. Ends with a question or invitation.

## Expected output

- Instagram carousel (4–6 slides) or single strong image
- Slide 1: visual hook with minimal text
- Slides 2–5: one moment from Mia's day each
- Caption 200–400 characters, warm and conversational
- 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end
- Visual direction must be specific enough to act on

## Validation criteria

- Visual direction is actionable (designer can execute without further questions)
- Carousel has 4–6 slides
- Caption is warm, not corporate
- Includes Loopin brand color (#5B4AE8) in visual direction
